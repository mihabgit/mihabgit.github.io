SAT Vocabulary Privacy Policy

SAT Vocabulary Prep Flashcards, by DewDrops · Last updated November 23, 2026

In short: we don't ask for your name, email or any account, and we don't run servers that collect your data. Your words, study progress and settings stay on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Who we are

This policy covers the Android app SAT Vocabulary Prep Flashcards (shown on your phone as SAT Vocabulary), package com.dewdrops.satvocabulary, published by DewDrops ("we"). Questions about privacy: dewdropsdev@gmail.com.

Data the app keeps on your device

To track your learning, the app stores on your device:

  • your study progress: the words you've studied, when each one is due for review, and your streak,
  • the words you bookmark,
  • your settings, such as your daily goal, reminder time and theme.

We never receive this data. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it from your device.

Features that use your phone

  • Daily reminder: if you turn it on, the app schedules a notification on your phone at the time you choose, and sets it again after your phone restarts. Android asks you before the app can show notifications.
  • Pronunciation: when you play a word's pronunciation, the app passes that word to your phone's text-to-speech engine (for example, Google's), which works under its own terms.
  • Sharing: when you share a Word of the Day or a quiz result, Android's share menu sends it to the app you pick. We don't see what you share.
  • Rating the app: the app may show Google Play's rating prompt. Your rating and review go to Google Play, not to us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens and an occasional full-screen ad between activities. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be estimated,
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps),
  • crash and performance diagnostics from the ad software.

Google may use this data to show personalized ads, depending on your choices and the law where you live. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. We don't sell your personal data and don't share it with anyone other than Google for ads.

Your choices

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent to personalized ads using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → Privacy options.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads).
  • You can turn the daily reminder off in the app, or block its notifications in Android settings.
  • You can study offline. No ads load without an internet connection.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in, and no access to your contacts, photos, microphone, SMS or location.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own, and no servers of our own that receive your data.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help.

Deleting your data

We don't hold any of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove data on your device, clear the app's data or uninstall it. To reset the data Google uses for ads, reset or delete your advertising ID as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because the app adds a feature such as in-app purchases or analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top.
