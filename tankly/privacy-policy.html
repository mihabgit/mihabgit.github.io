Tankly Privacy Policy

Tankly, by DewDrops · Effective 26 November 2026

Tankly is a fuel, mileage and service tracker for bikes and cars, made by DewDrops. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. Tankly has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never receive your data.

What Tankly stores

The app keeps the following on your phone:

  • Your vehicle: its name, number plate, type and starting odometer reading.
  • Your entries:
    • Fill-ups: date, odometer, fuel, quantity, price and station.
    • Expenses.
    • Service reminders.
    • Paper expiry dates, such as tax token, fitness, insurance, licence and route permit.
    • If you use rider mode, your ride-sharing earnings, trips, hours, distance and costs.
  • Your settings: language, theme, units, reminder choices and any fuel prices you type in.

All of this is stored only in Tankly's private storage on your phone, where other apps can't read it. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it.

What goes over the internet

Fuel prices. About once a day, when your phone is online, Tankly downloads the current government fuel price list. It's a small public file hosted on GitHub. The download doesn't send any of your entries or settings. Like any internet download, it does reveal some details to GitHub:

  • your phone's IP address;
  • the phone's model and Android version, which Android includes automatically.

GitHub handles these under the GitHub privacy statement. Tankly doesn't use these details, and we can't see who downloads the file.

The app doesn't connect to the internet for anything else.

Backups and exports you make

  • Settings → Back up to a file saves all your Tankly data to a file. Export to CSV saves your fill-ups, expenses and earnings to a file.
  • Both files go wherever you choose: your phone, Google Drive, or another app you send them to. Once saved there, the file is handled by that place's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Tankly never uploads these files itself.
  • The files aren't encrypted, and they include your number plate and any earnings, so keep them somewhere private.

Android backup

  • If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include Tankly's data in its automatic backup to your Google account. Your phone's settings control this, and Google runs the backup under its own privacy policy.
  • We can't access it.
  • You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you about papers that are about to expire and services that are due. The reminders are worked out on your phone. You can say no, and Tankly will show reminders only when you open it.
  • Internet: only for the fuel price download described above.

Tankly doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone or phone identifiers.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or crash reporting.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it:

  • One item: delete any entry or reminder in the app.
  • Everything: uninstall Tankly, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Tankly → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Copies elsewhere: delete any backup or CSV files you saved separately. An automatic Android backup in your Google account can be deleted from your Google account settings.

Because we never receive your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Tankly is made for vehicle owners and riders, not for children under 13. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and change the date at the top before that version of the app is released.
