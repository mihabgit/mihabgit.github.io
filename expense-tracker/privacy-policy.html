Expense Tracker Privacy Policy

Daily Expense Tracker & Budget, by DewDrops · Last updated November 23, 2026

In short: we don't ask for your name, email, or bank or card details, and we don't run servers that collect your data. Your expenses, budgets and loans stay on your phone. The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads. If you use the optional bKash summary, the app reads only bKash SMS messages, on your phone.

Who we are

This policy covers the Android app Daily Expense Tracker & Budget (shown on your phone as Expense Tracker), package com.mihab.expensetracker, published by DewDrops ("we"). Questions about privacy: dewdropsdev@gmail.com.

Data the app keeps on your device

Everything you enter is stored in a database on your device:

  • your expenses: amount, category, note and date,
  • your budgets, categories and Quick Add buttons,
  • subscriptions and free trials you add, and their reminder dates,
  • money you lend or borrow: the name you type for the other person, amounts, due dates and repayments,
  • your settings, such as currency, language, reminders and which cards appear on Home.

We never receive this data. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it from your device.

bKash summary and SMS (Bangladesh only)

In Bangladesh the app offers an optional bKash summary. If you open it and allow the SMS permission, the app reads only messages from the sender "bKash" in your SMS inbox, and shows a summary of those transactions. It does not read any other messages.

The summary is worked out on your phone each time you open it. The app doesn't save these messages, and never sends them to us or anyone else. You can turn the SMS permission off at any time in Android settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Features that use your phone

  • Your country: to pick a starting currency, and to show the bKash card only in Bangladesh, the app checks your SIM or mobile network country (or your phone's region setting) on your phone. This isn't sent anywhere.
  • Reminders: the evening reminder and the reminders before a free trial ends or a subscription renews are scheduled on your phone. Android asks you before the app can show notifications.
  • Home screen widget: if you add it, the widget shows today's and this month's spending on your home screen.
  • Export: exporting creates a CSV file of your expenses. You choose where it goes using Android's share menu. We never receive it.
  • Rating the app: the app may show Google Play's rating prompt. Your rating and review go to Google Play, not to us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be estimated,
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps),
  • crash and performance diagnostics from the ad software.

Google may use this data to show personalized ads, depending on your choices and the law where you live. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. We don't sell your personal data and don't share it with anyone other than Google for ads.

Your choices

  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads).
  • You can turn reminders off in the app, or block its notifications in Android settings.
  • You can turn the SMS permission off in Android settings at any time.
  • You can use the app offline. No ads load without an internet connection.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in, and no bank or card login.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone or location, and no SMS other than bKash messages, only when you use the bKash summary.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own, and no servers of our own that receive your data.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help.

Deleting your data

We don't hold any of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove your expenses and other data, clear the app's data or uninstall it; export a CSV first if you want to keep a copy. To reset the data Google uses for ads, reset or delete your advertising ID as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because the app adds a feature such as in-app purchases or analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top.
