Java Interview Questions Prep Privacy Policy

Java Interview Questions Prep, by DewDrops · Last updated November 23, 2026

In short: the app has no internet access, no ads, no analytics and no accounts. Everything you do in it stays on your phone, and we never receive any of it.

Who we are

This policy covers the Android app Java Interview Questions Prep (shown on your phone as Backend Interview Pro), package com.dewdrops.interviewprep, published by DewDrops ("we"). Questions about privacy: dewdropsdev@gmail.com.

No internet access

The app doesn't request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere: not to us, and not to anyone else.

Data the app keeps on your device

To run your study plan, the app stores on your device:

  • your progress: how you rated each card and when it's due for review, your streak and your mastery per topic,
  • the questions you bookmark or add to your review,
  • your study plan: the experience level and interview date you choose,
  • your settings, and whether you joined the Pro waitlist.

We never receive this data. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it from your device.

Pro waitlist

Joining the Pro waitlist only saves that choice on your phone. We don't receive it, and the app doesn't ask for your email.

Copying AI prompts

When you copy a mock interview prompt, the text goes to your phone's clipboard. If you paste it into an AI assistant, that service handles it under its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No ads, analytics or tracking, and no servers of our own.
  • No accounts or sign-in, and no access to your contacts, photos, microphone, SMS or location.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

We don't hold any of your data. To remove it from your device, clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because the app adds Pro purchases, ads or analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top.
